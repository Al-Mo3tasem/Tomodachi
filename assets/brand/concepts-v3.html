How to pick + the texture question answered

Compare D, E, F at primary-mark size first. The implied wearer in all three makes the scarf read as worn, not floating — which was the brief. Then check the favicon row: the simplified 16px versions show how each survives at small sizes.

On the texture: only Direction E (The Drape) demonstrates knit pattern + fabric noise — you can see it directly in the primary mark. Drag your eye close and you'll see the cable-knit stitch ribbing and the subtle organic break-up of the flat red. We can apply the same texture to D or F's scarves if you want — it's a per-direction choice. My recommendation: keep texture for E and F's scarves (medium and high stakes for the "scarf-as-real-object" reading), drop it for D where the strength is symmetry and silhouette, not fabric realism.

My take: Between D, E, F — I'd pick F (The Wrap-Around). Reasons: (1) the three-loop stack reads as a real lived-in scarf for everyone, regardless of whether they know the source; (2) the implied head + balanced composition fits a square icon natively, no special variants needed; (3) 友 stays the largest of the three new options, preserving brand-name readability; (4) we can add knit texture to it without overpowering. But if you want the strongest AoT signal, E (The Drape) is the answer. If you want quiet symmetry and dignity, D. Tell me D, E, or F — or "blend X with Y's Z" and I'll iterate.
