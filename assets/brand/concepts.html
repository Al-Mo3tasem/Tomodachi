Tomodachi — three logo directions, with the scarf

Each direction incorporates a stylized red scarf as a friendship motif. The scarf is a recognizable visual symbol (a fictional anime made it iconic; the form itself is in the cultural commons) — we use it without ever naming or referencing the show. To anyone who knows, it lands instantly. To anyone who doesn't, it still reads as a warm protective garment wrapping the character. Either reading works.

The new palette retires generic-startup indigo. Scarf crimson is now the primary brand color. Ink (near-black) replaces deep navy. Cream paper replaces cold off-white. The result: warmer, more grounded, with much more personality.

Strengths

Strongest reading of the scarf motif — there is no mistaking what's happening here. The ring shape doubles as a "circle of friends" reading for viewers unfamiliar with the reference. Survives well at all sizes because the ring + character composition is symmetrically anchored.

Tradeoffs

The most overt reference — if you ever want the brand to grow past the AoT-fan demographic into general audiences, the ring might still read fine, but the tails specifically signal "scarf." The compositional center of gravity is the ring, not 友, which is slightly less name-readable than B or C.

Strengths

Most cinematic. The horizontal scarf reads as motion — like the camera caught the moment the wind moved it. 友 stays the primary readable element, so the brand name remains the strongest signal at first glance. The compositional drift left-to-right also works beautifully for OG cards (1200×630 social-share previews need a wide composition; B is built for it).

Tradeoffs

Less symmetric, slightly more complex shape than A. The 16px favicon needs the most aggressive simplification of the three (the wavy banner flattens to a straight bar). The horizontal composition is harder to use as a square app icon — we'd need a separate stacked variant.

Direction C

The Embrace

A scarf curves under and around the 友 character in a gentle U-shape — like hands cupping something they want to protect. The character sits in the cradle of the scarf. The most quiet of the three, but also the most emotionally weighted. Reads as a hug before it reads as a scarf.

Strengths

The most emotionally resonant of the three. Reads as a hug before it reads as a scarf — meaning it survives the test of "does this work for someone who's never seen the show?" The asymmetric composition (mark sits above cradle) gives a natural vertical breathing rhythm. The two scarf tails dropping below add weight and motion without competing with 友 for attention.

Tradeoffs

The subtlest reference of the three — diehard AoT fans might miss it on a first glance. The taller composition (the cradle sits below 友) doesn't fit a square frame as cleanly as A; we'd need a stacked-vs-horizontal version split for icons. The cradle shape at very small sizes (16px) starts to read more like an empty bowl than a scarf.

My take, since you asked for premium: Direction B (The Banner).
