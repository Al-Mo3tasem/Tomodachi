Common questions

When is Tomodachi launching?
Tomodachi does not have a fixed launch date yet. We are holding release until the learning experience and Arabic quality meet the bar, and waitlist members will be invited to the closed beta first.
Is it free?
The first beta wave will be free. Later, Tomodachi will add a Pro tier for power-user features, while the free experience stays substantial.
Will there be a mobile app?
Tomodachi already works on mobile in the browser, and it supports home-screen installation as a PWA. Native iOS and Android apps are on the roadmap, but they are not the first release target.
Why Arabic-first?
Most Japanese-learning apps start in English and treat Arabic as a later layer. Tomodachi authors Arabic pedagogy natively — explanations written by people thinking in Arabic — so grammar, memory cues, and examples feel closer to the learner.
What Japanese levels will be covered?
Tomodachi starts with JLPT N5: kana, about 800 vocabulary items, about 100 kanji, and N5 grammar. N4 follows after that, while N3 and beyond remain roadmap items, not promises.
How is my data handled?
We store the minimum needed to run the product: email, display name, and game results. We do not sell data, the full privacy policy lives at /privacy, and deleting your account from Settings removes everything.
What will Pro cost?
Final pricing is not set. We are planning regional pricing around $2/month equivalent for Egypt, roughly 70 EGP, and around $4/month equivalent for Gulf countries, with affordability as the main goal.
I don't speak Egyptian Arabic — will it work for me?
Yes. All content ships in Modern Standard Arabic, so Arabic readers can use it regardless of dialect, with no Egyptian colloquialisms or region-specific wording that excludes Gulf, Levantine, or Maghrebi users.
